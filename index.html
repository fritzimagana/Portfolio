Approach

    Partner teams do not need a second status meeting to find the date. The plan is the place where design, content, broadcast, and engineering already agree.

    An open item goes to leadership with a recommendation and a fallback. The ask is a reaction, not a blank question.

    Meeting notes become the tracker and the brief. People keep the judgment about what is actually at risk.
