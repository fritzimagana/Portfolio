Approach

One source of truth

I bring plans, people, progress, and decisions into one connected view. A shared source of truth keeps stakeholders aligned, makes program health visible, and reduces the friction of managing information across multiple workstreams.

A point of view

I go beyond status reporting to provide perspective. I surface risks, identify gaps, challenge assumptions, and bring forward recommendations and options that help stakeholders make informed decisions and maintain momentum.

AI-enabled synthesis

I integrate AI into the work itself, using it to synthesize information, uncover patterns, structure ambiguity, and accelerate execution. I build practical AI-enabled workflows and agents that improve how information is processed, decisions are supported, and work gets delivered.

From ambiguity to action

I translate complexity into structure and forward movement. By clarifying objectives, connecting dependencies, establishing ownership, and defining next steps, I help teams move from uncertainty to coordinated execution.
